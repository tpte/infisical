Infisical — Mac Safari via Tailscale (no hi-yen CF)

Recommended right now (SSH LocalForward over Tailscale):
  http://127.0.0.1:18080/

Supervisor on Mac keeps:
  ssh -N -L 18080:127.0.0.1:80 msi
  and wakes WSL docker when needed
  log: /tmp/infisical-ts-forward.log

SITE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:18080

Direct Tailscale (when WSL stays up):
  http://msi:8080/
  (Windows portproxy 8080 → WSL :80 + firewall rule)

Known issue on MSI:
  Ubuntu-24.04 WSL is periodically poweroff/terminated (journal: InitTerminateInstanceInternal /
  systemctl poweroff). That kills docker for ~1–3 minutes. Direct :8080 then fails until WSL is awake again.
  The Mac SSH supervisor is the reliable Safari path until that terminate source is found/stopped.

Do not use infisical-vault.hi-yen.com for this.
